What we took from them for T1 / T2:

- **Rock:** light grey layered limestone (ambientCG Rock 023), not the brown
  sandstones of most rock libraries.
- **Meadows:** a dense, slightly yellowish grass (ambientCG Grass 004); the
  far colour comes from the material's mean, so valleys stay green, slopes
  under 60 % grass.
- **Gravel:** light grey river pebbles for the Rhine banks and the land
  classes "bare" and partly "water" (ambientCG Rocks 006); the dam tracks are
  OSM `surface=gravel|compacted` → gravel road (ambientCG Gravel 022).
- **Asphalt:** medium-grey fine aggregate (ambientCG Asphalt 015); the
  centre line stays the generated marking.
- **Farmland:** brown soil mixed with meadow in patches (fields are green
  most of the season).
