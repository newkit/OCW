**What the renders lack (for Phase 8):**

- Lava rock: black or red-brown and blocky. Today it is the grey `rock` class.
- Volcanic ash and pumice: the ochre caldera floor and the Montaña Blanca.
- Black sand: today it is the light `bare` class.
- Canary pines: long needles and an open forest. Today the forest has generic trees.
- Laurel forest: dense, dark broadleaf trees.
- Banana terraces and net houses: today these are orchard or farmland.
- Whitewashed houses with flat roofs.
