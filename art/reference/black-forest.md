What we took from them for T1 / T2:

- **Forest floor:** spruce needles and litter (Poly Haven Forest Leaves 04,
  from a pine forest), dark under the dense conifers.
- **Meadows / pastures:** the same grass as in Liechtenstein for now; the
  pale high pastures of the Feldberg are a job for the regional tint (T3:
  palettes measured from the orthophotos).
- **Wetland:** damp green moss and grass (ambientCG Ground 037), not mud.
- **Roads:** grey asphalt, worn asphalt where OSM says `smoothness` is
  intermediate or worse; forest roads are `track` with gravel.
