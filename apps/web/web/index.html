Open Cycling World browser preview

An open-source indoor cycling simulation, in your browser: ride solo on a test route or in Liechtenstein, with virtual power or your smart trainer over Bluetooth (Chrome or Edge). This is a development build – expect rough edges.

Choose a ride

Riding

  • Virtual power: ↑ more power, ↓ brake, Page Up / Down ±10 W; A autodrive, ← / → your line or lean.
  • Smart trainer, power meter, heart rate: the Devices panel, bottom right (Web Bluetooth: Chrome or Edge on Windows, macOS, ChromeOS and Android; on Linux behind a flag).
  • Menu: Esc (or the gamepad's Start) – events, workouts, the time and weather machines, settings, screenshots and the ride's FIT file. Mouse: the camera, C resets it.

Multiplayer, voice chat and sign-in are coming in the hosted version.
