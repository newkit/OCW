# Reference photos: aircraft (spec 14 Phase 18, 18G *Detail from the reference photos*)

Three photos the user took on an airport apron and gave the project on
2026-11-28 ("here are some images i took from airplanes and a helicopter.
use them to make the plane models more detailed and add one more helicopter
model"). They are the user's own photos, used with permission, for reference
only. **They are not copied into the repository**, as with the other boards
here: they show real airlines' names, logos and liveries, registration
marks and people of the ground crew, none of which belong in OCW. The models
take proportions, shapes and details from them – never a livery, a name, a
mark or a 1:1 copy of a real type; our liveries stay our own generic ones.

| Photo | What it shows | What the models take from it |
|---|---|---|
| `ref-1` – a narrow-body twinjet, three-quarter front left | Blended upward wing tips, large-diameter high-bypass engines close under the wing with the fan blades visible, nose gear with twin wheels and twin-wheel main gear, a four-panel windscreen with side windows, the cabin's window row with door outlines, fin and tailplane, the belly fairing; a boarding-stairs truck; a rescue helicopter far behind | `vehicles/airliner-twinjet`: the fuselage's taper and nose, the cockpit windows, the window row, the door, over-wing exit and cargo-door outlines, the belly fairing, the nacelles with inlet lip, fan face (spinner, 16 blades), fan and core nozzles and plug, pylons, flap-track fairings, sharklets, the dorsal fillet, the APU's tail cone, the gear with its doors |
| `ref-2` – a narrow-body twinjet from the front | Split winglets (an upper and a lower blade), engines slung lower and further forward with flatter nacelles, a more pointed nose; a boarding-stairs truck at the front door, a baggage tug and a fuel cart, ground crew in hi-vis vests | `vehicles/airliner-twinjet-split-winglet`: the longer fuselage, the pointed nose, the split winglets, the flatter and lower nacelles, the shorter gear |
| `ref-3` – a twin-turbine medium transport helicopter from the front | A five-blade main rotor drooping at rest, a big glazed nose, the engine cowlings above the cabin, external tanks and stores pylons on the sides, fixed tricycle gear, the tail rotor, a sand-tan finish | `vehicles/helicopter-medium-transport`: our own design of that class – glazed nose, boxy cabin with round windows and a sliding door, twin cowlings with side exhausts and the gearbox, five drooping blades, the boom with a swept pylon and a three-blade tail rotor on its left, fuel tanks and outrigger pylons with pods, fixed tricycle gear; a sand tan with a darker band (no markings) |

The apron vehicles (boarding stairs, baggage tug, fuel cart) are noted for
later props; they are not modelled yet.
